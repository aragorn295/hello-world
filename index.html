How it works

+ New Hand deals a new hand: posts antes & blinds, assigns positions (BTN, SB, BB, UTG…CO) clockwise from the dealer button.

The green-bordered "Next to act" card shows whose turn it is. Tap an action button (Fold / Check / Call / Bet / Raise / All-in) — for bet/raise/all-in, the amount field is pre-filled with a sensible default; edit and tap Record.

Tap any seat to record an action for that player out of order. Tap their hole-card slots to set hole cards; tap 📝 to edit reads.

The board at the top of each street takes 3 flop cards, 1 turn, 1 river — tap any slot to pick a card. Already-used cards are dimmed in the picker.

When all action is done, advance to Showdown, tap winner(s), describe the hand, and Finish — stacks settle, the button rotates, and the hand is archived in History.

State auto-saves to your browser. Use Export to back up.
